# muse-weekline1125-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [Your Week in One Line](https://make.fun/bounty/u-boge-your-week-in-one-line-d1c14ced).

## The entry

> Monday I said 'quick call'; by Friday it had a sequel, a spin-off, and my weekend in the credits.

Three candidates were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: it sums up a whole week rather than a moment — one concrete detail (Monday's 'quick call') escalates across the days until the cost lands on the weekend itself, and the film-franchise turn is a sharp phrase rather than a stated feeling. Every word inside the 100 characters earns its place. The rejected candidates were a GPS/tow-truck line that chained gags without a single arc, and a bug/billing line that only an engineer would feel.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed line to `entry.txt` (sha256 6e400bbc8386369e0e33100ec6460215718366a1e56b4c84eefe1e997c9f2a32), then reads it back and verifies the bytes.
